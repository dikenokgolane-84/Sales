# Sales Data Analysis Spreadsheet

## Overview
This spreadsheet contains sales data for a product, including quantity sold, sales, cost of sales, and gross profit. The data spans from 2013 to 2016 and includes various visualizations such as bar graphs, line graphs, and pie charts.

## Data
- *Quantity Sold*: Increasing trend from 2013 (100095) to 2016 (1250458)
- *Gross Profit*: Varied, with totals for each year and a Grand Total of 921,358,518
- *Sales*: Show growth, with Sum of Sales reaching 18009583.3

## Visualizations
- *Bar Graph*: Sales by month
- *Line Graph*: Cost of sale per month
- *Donut Chart*: % CHANGE IN PRICE PER YEAR
- *Pie Chart*: % GROSS PROFIT PER YEAR

## Notes
- Data is for analysis purposes only
- Actual data may vary based on source and updates
